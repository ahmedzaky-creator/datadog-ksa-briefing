Use case 01 · payments & banking

A mada payment fails for one bank's customers. Find it in minutes.

Investigation · same tags across every signalpress →
RUMPayment success is 99.8% overall, but 97.1% for cards from issuer:bank-b. The average hides it; the tag doesn't.
APM · SLOTraces for issuer:bank-b show 504s from switch-adapter after 8 s. The per-issuer SLO is burning 14× faster than budget, and On-Call pages the owner.
DATA STREAMS · DBMRetries pile up on Kafka topic payments.retry, with consumer lag rising from 4 s to 95 s. Database Monitoring shows Oracle core-banking queries normal, so the core is ruled out.
ACTA workflow caps retries and shows a "try another card" prompt for bank-b only. The incident timeline is captured for the regulator report, the Status Page is updated, and Bits AI drafts the postmortem.
The real failure mode

Public status pages of payment providers operating in KSA record intermittent declines from specific issuing banks (2024–2026), not total outages. That's exactly what averages hide.
